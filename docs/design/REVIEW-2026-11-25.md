## Summary

The first launch reproduces the prototype's numbers (stage at 56 pt and 48 % of the height, words
92 pt above the bottom, caption 64 pt above the stage's bottom, 16 pages, 16 % / 350 pt/s swipe
thresholds) and its sky, stage and caption crossfades are correctly tied to the finger on the UI
thread. What the owner feels as "not the prototype" is real and comes from five things: **the words
slide away with the page instead of staying pinned and crossfading**, **the page order and three
skies were changed** (warm → green → warm flash), **the copy was rewritten on four pages**, **the
demos are static or pop in after the page settles**, and **nothing in the garden is alive** (the
prototype animates water, fountains and glow). App-wide, motion is sparse and flat: press feedback
is a 10 % opacity snap everywhere, progress never animates, the reader bars and listening results
swap without transition, and haptics exist only in the reader.

Thesis of the screen: _"your reading grows a garden, and neglect dries it"_; the one thing it should
be remembered by is the garden growing and dying under your thumb. Today that moment exists as a
crossfade between still images, so it does not land.

**Overall rating: Needs work** (no Critical finding; several High). Scores 7 / 7 / 5 / 7 / 5:
**sent back to its developers** (two dimensions under 7).

## Critical

None found. Accessibility (Lens 1) holds: the words scroll at the largest text sizes, VoiceOver
reads one description per scene and an adjustable page control, reduce motion stops every loop,
and computed contrast passes (table in Lens 1 notes below).

## What works

- The pager: 1:1, velocity-seeded spring, rubber band, interruptible, RTL mirrored
  (`swipe-paging.ts`); better than the prototype's fixed curve. Keep.

  derived from one position on the UI thread: the right architecture for scroll-driven motion.

  previews hidden at large text and on short screens.
- The real rosette and the real follow page as demos (owner's decision): the onboarding shows the
  app the person will use.
- Launch animation: springs, stagger, ease-out, a reduced timeline.
