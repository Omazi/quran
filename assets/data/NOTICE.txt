QURAN DATA: SOURCES AND NOTICES

These files are generated by tools/quran-data and are never edited by hand.
Where each source comes from, what is established and what is not (in French):
docs/DATA_SOURCES.md


verses/uthmani.json
-------------------
The text of each verse is taken from the column "aya_text" of hafsData_v18.json,
the Hafs data published by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex
(Madinah) with version 0.18 of its font "KFGQPC HAFS Uthmanic Script". The copy
comes from github.com/ibnhazm/kfgqpc (commit 25f2f8886d6ad0b47190f252d9d7fb1e296f3231),
which mirrors the Complex's developer page.

Exactly two things are removed from the printed verse, and nothing else is
changed: the no-break space and verse number at its end, and the no-break space
after the hizb sign. The text is made to be displayed with that version of that
font (assets/fonts, with its own notice).

No licence text for the data file itself was found in the mirror. The font
shipped with it carries the Complex's licence: see assets/fonts/NOTICE.txt.


verses/imlaei.json
------------------
The text of each verse is copied unchanged from the column "aya_text_emlaey" of
the same file, hafsData_v18.json of the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing
Complex (same mirror, same commit as above). It is the verse in the everyday
(imla'i) spelling, without vowel marks, as the Complex publishes it. Nothing is
added, removed or changed. The same notice as for verses/uthmani.json applies.

verses/uthmani-minimal.json.

package quran-qcf4 1.1.0 (layout of the Medina mushaf, 1441 AH edition).
