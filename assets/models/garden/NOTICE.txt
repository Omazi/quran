Garden model (level 1): provenance and licences
================================================

What this file is
-----------------
assets/models/garden/level-1/garden.glb is the level-1 garden in 3D, shown by the app's
GardenView3D (decision D40). It is baked by tools/garden-3d from the same models, layout,
materials and light as the garden pictures (assets/images/garden, decision D35): one binary
glTF file, 4.9 MB (1.6 MB once compressed in the app package), with:

- the triangles of every piece the pictures show, merged by material or instanced, each tagged
  with the stages (elements, water, foliage) where it is drawn;
- the textures of the models, reduced to 256 or 128 pixels, as raw RGBA pixels (no image file);
- no animation, no camera, no light (the app sets the pictures' light).

Not from a pack
---------------
The earth block, its border, the beds, the paving, the channels, the basin, the water, the
spring pools, the waterfall's water and pool, the mottled texture of the earth and the lawn,
and the drought tint are the picture tool's own plain geometry and shader (D35). They belong to
the project.

Gardens of levels 2 to 7 (decision D46) - downloaded packs, not in the app
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
dist/garden-art/level-N.gardenpack (N = 2 to 7) holds the garden of one level: its baked GLB, its
pictures and growth sprites, and a copy of this notice. The packs are built by tools/garden-3d
(levels/make_levels.py, bake.py, render.py, pack.mjs), published on the branch "garden-art" of the
project's repository, downloaded by the app when a level needs one and checked against the SHA-256
of src/data/garden-art/garden-art.json. Same two sources and licences as level 1, same mirrors
(the authors' sites, quaternius.com and kenney.nl, refused the connection from the machine that
built them on 26 November 2026: to download again from them before a store release).

  Quaternius, "Ultimate Stylized Nature Pack" - CC0 1.0 (as above). Models: BirchTree_1 to _4,
  Bush, Bush_Flowers, Bush_Large, Bush_Large_Flowers, Bush_Small, Bush_Small_Flowers,
  Flower_1_Clump to Flower_5_Clump, Grass_Large, Grass_Large_Extruded, Grass_Small, MapleTree_1,
  _3, _4, NormalTree_1 to _5, PalmTree_1 to _5, Petals_1 to _3, PineTree_1 to _5, Plant_1,
  Plant_2, Plant_Flowers, Rock_1 to _5, and their textures (Leaves_BW, MapleTree_Leaves_BW and
  Rocks_Desert among them). The blossom of the cherry trees and the night garden's white
  blossom are the pack's greyscale leaves tinted by the tool.

  Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - CC0 1.0 (as above). Castle Kit: tower-square-arch, wall-narrow.
  Fantasy Town Kit: fountain-round, fountain-square, fountain-square-detail, hedge-large,
  hedge-large-gate, lantern, pillar-stone, pillar-wood, roof-high-point, watermill-wide.
  Furniture Kit: books, pillow, pillowBlue, pillowLong, rugRectangle, rugRound, rugSquare,
  sideTable. Graveyard Kit: candle-multiple, lantern-candle, lantern-glass, stone-wall,
  stone-wall-column. Holiday Kit: lantern, lantern-hanging. Nature Kit: bridge_wood,
  bridge_woodNarrow, bridge_woodRound, campfire_stones, cliff_steps_stone,
  cliff_waterfallTop_stone, fence_gate, fence_simple, lily_large, lily_small, log_large,
  log_stack, mushroom_redGroup, mushroom_tanGroup, path_stone, path_stoneCircle, pot_large,
  pot_small, stump_roundDetailed, tent_detailedOpen. Pirate Kit: boat-row-small, crate,
  structure, structure-fence-sides. Survival Kit: bedroll, structure-canvas, structure-roof.
  Some are recoloured by the tool (one plain colour, or their own colours multiplied).

  Not from a pack: the plots, the ground, paths, terraces, water, waterfalls' water, the glow
  under the night garden's lanterns and the moon on its pool are the tool's plain shapes; the
  night garden's light is the tool's. They belong to the project.
