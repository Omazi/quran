Time zone coordinates: provenance and licence
============================================

What it is
----------
src/domain/sun/zone-coordinates.ts gives, for each time zone name of the IANA time zone
database, the latitude and longitude of the place the database names it after (America/Toronto:
Toronto). The app uses it to place the sun from the phone's time zone alone, without asking for
the location (docs/DECISIONS.md D51). It is written by tools/sun-zones/build.mjs.

Source
------
IANA time zone database (tz, "tzdata"), version written in the generated file's header (2026e
when this file was written), files zone.tab, zone1970.tab and tzdata.zi, as installed by
Ubuntu's tzdata package in /usr/share/zoneinfo. https://www.iana.org/time-zones
