App icon: provenance
====================

Files: assets/expo.icon/ (icon.json and Assets/: leaf.svg, water.svg, word.svg) and, in
assets/images/, icon.png, android-icon-foreground.png, android-icon-background.png,
android-icon-monochrome.png, favicon.png, splash-icon.png (decision D38, version 6).

They are drawn by tools/app-icon/make_icon.py from tools/app-icon/word.svg.

The word
--------
word.svg holds the outlines of the word جنتك set in the typeface Gulzar (Regular, version 1.000),
shaped by HarfBuzz and outlined by fontTools (make_icon.py --outline). The letters are the
typeface's own, unchanged: not redrawn, not distorted; only their colours differ (the dots are
coloured as water). The font file itself is not in the repository and does not ship in the app.

Variant: the Arabic final kaf (not in the app; waiting for the owner's choice, D38)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
word-arabic.svg holds the same Gulzar outlines, unchanged, plus one more shape: the small mark the
Arabic kaf (ك) carries inside, which Gulzar's Persian-style final kaf (ک) does not have. The mark
is the glyph SharetKafNS of the typeface Noto Nastaliq Urdu (variable, at its Bold weight, 700),
outlined by fontTools, scaled by 0.81 and placed inside Gulzar's kaf; its shape is the
typeface's own, not redrawn (make_icon.py, KAF_MARK). make_icon.py --kaf arabic uses it.

The leaf and the background
---------------------------
The leaf (two arcs and a slit) and every colour are set in make_icon.py. They belong to the
project.

Tools used, not shipped in the app: HarfBuzz through uharfbuzz (Apache-2.0; HarfBuzz itself is
under the "Old MIT" licence), fontTools (MIT), rsvg-convert from librsvg (LGPL-2.1-or-later, run
as a separate program, nothing linked or copied), Python with Pillow (MIT-CMU) and numpy
(BSD-3-Clause).

Version 5 of the icon (a 3D render of a tree by a stream) was made by an earlier make_icon.py and
tools/app-icon/scene.html; both are in the git history (commit c3e0467).
