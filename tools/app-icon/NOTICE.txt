App icon: provenance
====================

Files: assets/expo.icon/ (icon.json and Assets/: tree.png, water.png, ground.png) and, in
assets/images/, icon.png, android-icon-foreground.png, android-icon-background.png,
android-icon-monochrome.png, favicon.png, splash-icon.png (decision D38).

They are renders, made on 25 November 2026 by tools/app-icon/make_icon.py from the scene
tools/app-icon/scene.html. They are pictures: no 3D file is in the app.

The tree
  Quaternius (quaternius.com), Platformer Game Kit, model Tree_Fruit.glb

  Licence: CC0 1.0, as given for this kit by the mirror's LICENSES.md ("Platformer Game Kit |
  CC0-1.0 | https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateplatformer.html", checked by the mirror's author
  against quaternius.com on 2026-10-10). The mirror's MIT licence covers its own code only.
  File used: the third-party GitHub mirror trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase, commit
  0128ca510d653bb04d6d1e4885bd8890b5050b4f. Its colours were replaced by flat ones in the scene.

Not from a pack
  The lawn, its earth band, the pool and its edge are plain geometry made by the scene; the
  colours and the light are the scene's. They belong to the project.

Tools used to make them, not shipped in the app: three.js 0.187.0 (MIT), run in headless
Chromium through Playwright (Apache-2.0), Python with Pillow (MIT-CMU) and numpy (BSD-3-Clause).
